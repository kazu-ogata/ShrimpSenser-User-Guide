ShrimpSense

User Guide

Shrimp Counting & Feed Optimization System
Follow the steps below to safely operate the ShrimpSense system.

01 Before You Start

Step 1

Fill the Water Tank

📍 Water Tank
  • Locate the Water Tank door at the lower-right compartment.
  • Open the door labeled “Water Tank.”
  • Fill the internal water tank with clean water suitable for shrimp.
  • Make sure there is enough water for the waterflow system to operate properly.
Step 2

Check the Main Switch

📍 Lower-left compartment
  • Locate the lower-left compartment door.
  • Open the door to access the electrical box.
  • The Main Switch is on the left side of the electrical box.
  • Make sure the switch is set to OFF.
Step 3

Connect the Power

  • Make sure the Main Switch is set to OFF.
  • Plug the prototype's power cord into a suitable wall outlet or power source.
  • Once the power cord is connected, you may proceed to turn the Main Switch to ON.

02 Power On

Step 4

Turn On the Main Switch

  • Manually flip the external Main Switch to ON.
  • The Raspberry Pi and touchscreen console will automatically boot up.
  • Wait for the touchscreen console to finish starting before proceeding.

03 Prepare the Feed Dispenser

Step 5

Add Feed

📍 Feed Dispenser
  • Locate the Feed Dispenser on the left side of the compartment.
  • On the touchscreen console, open the feeding wheel interface.
  • Spin the feeding wheel until the opening is positioned at the top.
  • Manually pour the feed into the dispenser through the opening.
  • Make sure the feed is loaded before starting.

04 Choose Counting Option

Step 6

Choose Your Counting Option

Option 1 — Count All Shrimp Use this option if you want the system to count all the shrimp you will load. You may proceed without setting a specific target count.
Option 2 — Set a Target Count Use this option if you only need to count a specific number of shrimp. Enter the desired target shrimp count on the touchscreen console. The system will count the shrimp until the target count is reached.
Step 7

Start the Waterflow System

  • Press the button on the touchscreen console to start the waterflow system.
  • Wait for the water to flow properly before loading the shrimp.

05 Load the Shrimp

Step 8

Load the Post-Larval Shrimp

📍 Loading Area
⚠️ Important Load the shrimp gradually. Do not pour all the shrimp into the Loading Area at once.
  • Locate the Loading Area at the top-left section of the prototype.
  • This is the 2nd station, labeled “Loading Area.”
  • Pour the post-larval shrimp into the loading area gradually.
  • Loading too many shrimp at once may cause the system to become blocked.
  • Allow the shrimp to pass smoothly through the slide and imaging station.
Step 9

Automatic Counting

  • The system will automatically detect and count the shrimp as they pass through the imaging station.
  • Continue loading the shrimp gradually.
  • If you selected Count All Shrimp, continue loading until all the shrimp you want to count have passed through the system.
  • If you selected Set a Target Count, the system will continue counting until the target shrimp count is reached.

06 View Results

Step 10

Check the Shrimp Count and Biomass

  • Check the final shrimp count displayed on the touchscreen console.
  • The system will calculate the biomass based on the counted shrimp.
  • Review the displayed biomass result before proceeding.
Step 11

Check the Feed Recommendation

  • The system will provide a recommended amount of feed based on the calculated biomass.
  • Review the recommended feed amount displayed on the touchscreen console.

07 Dispense Feed

Step 12

Dispense the Recommended Feed

  • When you are ready to dispense the recommended feed, press the Feed Dispense button on the touchscreen console.
  • The feed dispenser will release the recommended amount of feed.
  • Wait until the dispensing process is complete.
Step 13

Collect the Dispensed Feed

📍 Collection Box
  • Locate the collection box underneath the Feed Dispenser.
  • Once dispensing is complete, carefully remove the collection box.
  • Collect the dispensed feed from the box.

08 Collect the Shrimp

Step 14

Collect the Counted Shrimp

📍 Collection Area
  • At the far-right end of the waterflow system, locate the area labeled “Collection Area.”
  • The catcher/strainer is located in this area.
  • The catcher has a built-in water storage area and wire mesh at the top corners.
  • Carefully lift the catcher/strainer to collect the counted shrimp.
  • The shrimp will be collected together with water to help keep them safe.
  • Transfer the shrimp to the desired container or pond.

09 Shut Down

Step 15

Shut Down the Raspberry Pi

  • Use the touchscreen console to properly shut down the Raspberry Pi.
  • Wait until the system has completely shut down.
Step 16

Turn Off the Main Switch

  • Set the Main Switch to OFF.
Step 17

Unplug the Prototype

  • Unplug the prototype's power cord from the wall outlet or power source.

10 Extended Storage

For 3–5 Days or Longer

Prepare the Prototype for Extended Non-Use

  • Remove the remaining water from the water tank and waterflow system.
  • Remove any remaining feed from the Feed Dispenser.
  • Clean the prototype and remove any shrimp or feed residue.
  • Dry the prototype thoroughly before storing or leaving it unused.
  • Keep the prototype clean and dry to help prevent buildup, blockage, and other issues.

⚠️ Important Reminders

  • Always make sure the Main Switch is OFF before plugging in the power cord.
  • Always use water suitable for shrimp.
  • Always load post-larval shrimp gradually to prevent blockage.
  • Do not pour all the shrimp into the Loading Area at once.
  • Review the shrimp count, biomass, and feed recommendation on the touchscreen after counting.
  • Only press the Feed Dispense button when you are ready to dispense the recommended feed.
  • Properly shut down the Raspberry Pi before turning OFF the Main Switch.
  • Always unplug the prototype from the wall outlet or power source after shutting it down.
  • If the prototype will not be used for several days, remove the water and feed, then clean and dry the prototype.
